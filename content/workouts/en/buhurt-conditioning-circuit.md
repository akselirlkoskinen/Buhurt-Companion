# Buhurt Conditioning Circuit

A full-body conditioning circuit designed to build the endurance and explosive power you need in armour. No equipment required — suitable for indoor or outdoor training.

## Overview

3 rounds of 7 stations with minimal rest. The work intervals match typical buhurt round lengths so your body learns to recover quickly.

**Format:** 45 sec work · 15 sec rest · 90 sec between rounds

---

## Warm-up (5 min)

- Arm circles × 20 (forward + backward)
- Hip rotations × 20
- Bodyweight squats × 15 (slow, controlled)
- High knees × 30 sec
- Lateral shuffles × 30 sec

---

## Circuit (3 rounds)

### Station 1 — Armour Squat Simulation
Squat down, hold at the bottom for 2 sec, drive back up explosively.

**Target:** 12 reps

> *Builds leg strength for pushing and grappling from a low stance.*

---

### Station 2 — Bear Hug Sprawls
Stand with feet shoulder-width apart. Drop into a sprawl (chest to floor), push back up, then simulate a bear-hug grab by clasping your hands in front and rotating your torso left and right × 4.

**Target:** 8 full cycles

> *Trains the sprawl-and-clinch movement pattern common in 1v1.*

---

### Station 3 — Diagonal Chops
Lunge forward-left, swing both arms diagonally across your body as if bringing a weapon down (controlled, no weight). Return and repeat to the right.

**Target:** 10 each side

> *Develops rotational power for weapon strikes and shield work.*

---

### Station 4 — Isometric Wall Push
Stand arm's length from a wall. Press both palms flat against it and push as hard as you can for the full 45 seconds.

**Target:** Maximum tension throughout

> *Strengthens the pushing muscles used in shield contact.*

---

### Station 5 — Lateral Step & Sprawl
Take 3 lateral steps right → sprawl → up → 3 lateral steps left → sprawl → up.

**Target:** Continuous for 45 sec

> *Footwork agility combined with floor recovery.*

---

### Station 6 — Hip Hinge Row Simulation
Hinge at the hips (as if picking something up), clasp hands, then drive hips forward and pull elbows back explosively.

**Target:** 15 reps

> *Posterior chain — essential for posture under armour weight.*

---

### Station 7 — Plank Hold with Head Turn
Hold a forearm plank. Every 5 seconds, turn your head left then right (simulating checking flanks in melee).

**Target:** Full 45 sec

> *Core stability and neck mobility under fatigue.*

---

## Cool-down (5 min)

- Child's pose — 60 sec
- Hip flexor stretch (kneeling lunge) — 30 sec each side
- Chest-opener (clasp hands behind back, lift) — 30 sec
- Seated hamstring stretch — 30 sec each side
- Slow diaphragmatic breathing — 60 sec

---

## Scaling

| Level | Adjustment |
|-------|-----------|
| Beginner | 2 rounds · 35 sec work · 25 sec rest |
| Advanced | 4 rounds · 50 sec work · 10 sec rest · add 5 kg vest |

---

## Notes

- Rest as needed between stations if you are training in full armour.
- Hydrate before and after — armour significantly raises perceived exertion.
- This circuit pairs well with a sparring session on the same day (do circuit first).
